About

I'm a double major in Business Administration and Computer Science at Korea University (GPA 4.25/4.5). I'm currently a research assistant at the University of Washington's Social Futures Lab.

I study how AI and online platforms affect public discourse, and how these systems might better support informed reasoning and cooperation across sociopolitical divides.

Going forward, I want to use LLMs to identify recurring frames and network models to trace how they circulate, and LLM agent simulations to explore whether AI-generated content changes how divisive narratives spread. My long-term goal is to turn this evidence into policy research on misinformation, polarization, and responsible AI adoption.
